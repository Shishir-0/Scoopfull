What comes with the name

Your parlour runs on the same system as ours.

  • Product. Every flavour, dessert and topping, delivered in refrigerated vehicles through simple indents.
  • Training. A 10-day programme on your counter. Nobody serves a customer without scoring 95 out of 100.
  • Audits. Two scheduled and one surprise audit every month, scored and shared with you.
  • Pricing and menu. Set centrally and revised with ingredient costs, so the brand means the same thing everywhere.
  • Delivery and POS. Zomato and Swiggy listings under the Scoopful name, live from day one.
  • Launch. A trainer on site through opening, plus a launch campaign.

Questions people ask first

Which model should I choose?

FOCO if you want a hands-off investment and accept a slower payback. FOFO if you want to be involved and keep the full upside.

In FOCO, do I ever pay for losses?

No. Scoopful pays rent, salaries and every running cost. Your payout is calculated on sales, not profit. You pay the rent deposit, which is refunded when the lease ends.

Is there a guaranteed return?

No. FOCO payouts move with sales, and FOFO profit depends on the parlour. We will not promise a number we can’t control.

What is the FOFO royalty charged on?

Dine-in and takeaway sales before GST, and the payout amount on Zomato and Swiggy orders: 6% royalty plus a 2% brand fund.

Can I change the menu, prices or suppliers?

No. Scoopful supplies everything and sets the menu and prices for all parlours. That is how every outlet tastes the same.

How long is the agreement?

Five years, renewable. FOFO has a three-year lock-in; FOCO investors can exit early by transferring their investment, with Scoopful holding the first right to buy.
