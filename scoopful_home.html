“We didn't start Scoopful to sell ice cream. We started it to give people a reason to smile, one scoop at a time.”

Revanth Dinesh, Founder

  1. The beginning

    A bold idea takes root

    Revanth bootstrapped Scoopful from personal savings, with one vision: a premium ice cream brand people genuinely remember. Months went into recipes and systems before a single cup was sold.

  2. 2 March 2025

    Official launch

    Scoopful opened and sold out in the first week. Demand exceeded supply from day one.

  3. After launch

    Built to survive

    Cold storage challenges and marketplace growing pains. Scoopful faced them and rebuilt stronger.

  4. Today

    4.8 stars and growing

    200+ Google reviews, and now opening to franchise partners. The next chapter is scale.

How it's made

  • Real ingredients. Alphonso mango, Van Houten cocoa and fruit purées instead of flavour shortcuts.
  • Small batches. Made to our own recipes, with a Scoopful person at every production run.
  • Weighed, not guessed. 80 g a scoop, so it's the same every time you come back.
